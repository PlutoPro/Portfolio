About Me

Hi, I'm Chandler Higginbotham, a software engineer and infrastructure specialist based in West Virginia. I'm currently pursuing my B.S. in Software Engineering at Western Governors University with a focus on Java. My expertise bridges the gap between low-level systems programming and cybersecurity. I have a deep passion for penetration testing and offensive security, which drives me to constantly build and refine custom security tools, often utilizing languages like Golang and Rust. I thrive on deconstructing complex systems to uncover their vulnerabilities, and I love the creative challenge of developing utilities that automate and enhance the testing process. This hands-on security mindset is backed by a comprehensive suite of IT certifications, including CompTIA CySA+ and PenTest+.

When I'm not writing code, soldering custom ESP32 microcontrollers, or developing indie games in Unreal Engine, I love spending time with my wife and son. You can usually find me dialing in the perfect two-zone outdoor grilling setup or playing heavy metal drums—where I've found that mastering complex musical polyrhythms translates surprisingly well to understanding system-level software synchronization.
