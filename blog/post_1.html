Init Portfolio and Blog

Creating a portfolio website from a beginner's perspective

November 18, 2026

While reflecting on past accomplishments is rewarding, designing a centralized platform to showcase them provides a much stronger sense of ownership.

Building the site’s foundation in HTML and shaping the visual experience with CSS reinforced just how powerful and versatile these core web technologies can be.

To ensure the site remains lightweight and performant, I structured the blog around a reusable static HTML template. My active certifications are currently featured in the footer, with plans to migrate them to a dedicated credentials section as the site grows.
